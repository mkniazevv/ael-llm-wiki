# Understanding the use of AI among small businesses (JPMorganChase Institute)

## TL;DR

- Small firms that pay for AI tools are growing fast, and newer firms adopt faster: the 2025 cohort reached 10 percent adoption in about 6 months, the 2019 cohort in about 77 months. (Figure 2)
- Spending shifted to the bottom tier: in 2025, 63 percent of paying firms were in the bottom tier, 22 percent in the medium tier and 16 percent in the top tier. (Figure 7)
- The information sector leads (39.3 percent by the end of 2025); professional services reached about 30.3 percent. (Figure 10)
- Generative AI is the largest share of paid AI services in every industry shown, 54 percent in professional services. (Figure 11)

## Key claims

- Cohort speed: 10 percent adoption after about 77 months for the 2019 cohort and after 6 months for the 2025 cohort. Figure 2.
- Spending tiers in 2025: 63, 22 and 16 percent (bottom, medium, top). Figure 7. Tier limits are defined in the description of Figure 7.
- Adoption by industry, end of 2025: information 39.3 percent, professional services about 30.3 percent, educational services about 29.5 percent. Figure 10.
- Generative AI share of paid AI services in 2025: professional services 54 percent, information 49 percent. Figure 11.
- The share of generative AI users paying every month rose to 30.8 percent in 2025. Figure A2.

## Quotes

> firms in the information sector exhibit the highest AI adoption rates throughout the period, increasing from approximately 5 percent in 2019 to 39.3 percent by the end of 2025.

> In 2025, 63 percent of firms are in the bottom tier, 22 percent in the medium tier, and 16 percent in the top tier.

> By 2025, 30.8 percent of generative AI users pay for an AI service every month.

## Caveats

- **Measure:** adoption means paying for an AI tool, seen in payment data. Free use is not counted.
- **Where:** almost certainly US small businesses (spending in US dollars; employer and nonemployer firms), but the captured text does not say so.
- **Publisher and date:** not in the captured text. The organisation is taken from the web address; the researcher's note in the agent says JPMC. No publication date found; data run to the end of 2025.
- **Values are read from chart descriptions**, so they are approximate. One description says the paying share increases from 4 to 3.7 percent, which is a decrease; check the original chart.
- **Industries are broad US groups**; the source does not say where marketing or advertising agencies fall.
- **Independence:** published by a bank's research institute using its own customers' payment data.
- **Team appraisal (rubric v1.0, outside this wiki):** the AI suggested 4/10 (Tier C); Samsul raised authority from 0 to 2, giving 6/10, Tier B (agent run RUN-003, 27 Sep 2026).
