# Bedrijven gebruiken AI vaakst voor marketing of verkoop (CBS, 2025)

*English title (working translation): Businesses use AI most often for marketing or sales.* The source is in Dutch. Quotes below are the Dutch original; the English lines in italics are the team's working translation and are not evidence.

## TL;DR

- Dutch businesses of every size use more AI than before; the rise is strongest for firms with 50 to 250 persons employed (20 percent in 2023 to 45 percent in 2025). (P1)
- Marketing or sales is the most common purpose: 35 percent of AI-using businesses use it for that. (section *Ruim 1 op 3 gebruikt AI voor marketing of verkoop*)
- The AI technologies used most are text analysis (text mining, 12 percent) and text generation (natural language generation, 8 percent). (section *Vooral meer textmining en spraakherkenning*)
- Information and communication firms use AI most (54 percent); specialised business services, which include advertising and market research, also use it relatively often. (section on industries)
- Three quarters of businesses do not use AI and have not considered it. Of those that considered it, 73 percent name lack of experience; privacy (49 percent) and legal consequences (42 percent) also play a role. (last section)

## Key claims

- AI use rose for small and large businesses; strongest rise for 50 to 250 persons employed, from 20 percent (2023) to 45 percent (2025). First paragraph.
- Table *Gebruik AI, bedrijfsgrootte*: the row for 10 to 50 persons employed reads 271911, most likely 27 percent (2025), 19 percent (2024) and 11 percent (2023). The column breaks were lost in capture, so this reading needs checking on the live page or in StatLine. 2025 figures are provisional.
- Of AI-using businesses, 35 percent use AI for marketing or sales, 32 percent for administration or management tasks, 25 percent for R&D or innovation, 4 percent for logistics. Section *Ruim 1 op 3*.
- Almost half of businesses in trade and about 40 percent in information and communication, and in rental and other business services, use AI for marketing or sales. Same section.
- Text mining rose from 4 percent of businesses (2023) to 12 percent (2025); natural language generation is at 8 percent. Section on technologies.
- 54 percent of businesses in information and communication use one or more AI technologies, up from 28 percent in 2023. Specialised business services (accountancy, advertising and market research, industrial design) use AI relatively often. Section on industries.
- Reasons for not using AI: lack of experience (73 percent of firms that considered AI), privacy (49 percent), legal consequences (42 percent), not useful (15 percent). Last section.

## Quotes

> Van de bedrijven die software of systemen op basis van AI gebruiken, doet 35 procent dit voor marketing of verkoop.

*Of the businesses that use software or systems based on AI, 35 percent do so for marketing or sales.*

> Ook bedrijven in de specialistische zakelijke dienstverlening (zoals accountancy, reclame en marktonderzoek, en industriële ontwerp en vormgeving) en financiële dienstverlening gebruiken relatief vaak AI.

*Businesses in specialised business services (such as accountancy, advertising and market research, and industrial design and styling) and financial services also use AI relatively often.*

> Bedrijven die dit wel hebben overwogen maar het toch niet gebruiken, noemen gebrek aan ervaring als belangrijkste reden (73 procent).

*Businesses that did consider it but still do not use it cite lack of experience as the main reason (73 percent).*

> Ook privacy en onduidelijkheid over de juridische gevolgen, zoals aansprakelijkheid bij schade, spelen een rol: 49 procent van de bedrijven noemt privacy als reden om geen AI-technologie te gebruiken, 42 procent noemt juridische gevolgen.

*Privacy and lack of clarity about the legal consequences, such as liability for damage, also play a role: 49 percent of businesses cite privacy as a reason not to use AI technology, 42 percent cite legal consequences.*

## Caveats

- **Who published it:** the Dutch national statistics office, reporting its own survey (StatLine table *ICT-gebruik bedrijven*). No commercial interest, but an official source is not automatically independent; no method, sample or response rate is in the release.
- **Scope:** all sectors and sizes in the Netherlands. Advertising is not reported separately; it sits inside specialised business services with accountancy and design. Nothing here is about Gelderland.
- **Marketing or sales** means firms using AI for their own marketing, not agencies serving clients.
- **Provisional:** the 2025 figures are marked *voorlopige cijfers*.
- **Base unclear:** the 49 and 42 percent barrier figures say *van de bedrijven*; the sentence before refers to firms that considered AI. The source does not settle which base is meant.
- **Capture:** tables lost their column breaks; each table appears twice in the capture.
