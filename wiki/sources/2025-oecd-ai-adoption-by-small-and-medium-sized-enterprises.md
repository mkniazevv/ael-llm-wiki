# AI adoption by small and medium-sized enterprises (OECD, 2025)

## TL;DR

- SMEs use AI much less than large firms across the G7 and the OECD: 11.9 percent of firms with 10 to 49 employees against 40 percent of firms with 250 or more. (p. 10; executive summary p. 6)
- ICT and professional services lead adoption by sector. (executive summary p. 6)
- Among SMEs that use generative AI, only 29 percent use it in their core activities. (p. 11)
- The paper proposes a taxonomy of AI adopters, from AI Novices (embedded tools for peripheral tasks) to AI Champions (AI across operations and strategy). (p. 6, pp. 17 to 21)
- Four enablers are named: connectivity, AI-enabling inputs, skills and finance. (executive summary p. 6)

## Key claims

- Across the OECD, 40 percent of firms with 250 or more employees used AI in 2024 or the latest year, against 20.4 percent of firms with 50 to 249 and 11.9 percent of firms with 10 to 49 employees. p. 10.
- The share of firms using AI rose from 5.6 percent to 14 percent across OECD members between 2020 and 2024. Executive summary, p. 6.
- Among SMEs using generative AI, 29 percent report using it in core activities. p. 11.
- Two non-representative OECD surveys of G7 SMEs (2023 and 2024) found generative AI was the most used type of AI; the authors read this as a hint that low cost and skill barriers help uptake for basic uses. p. 15.
- Complexity of AI use runs from AI embedded in everyday software and off-the-shelf models to tailored systems and frontier AI. p. 17.
- Attitudes to generative AI did not appear to be a barrier: 86 percent of SMEs reported a neutral or favourable attitude. p. 39.
- SMEs in all adopter categories report problems with accuracy, harmful content and legal uncertainty. Executive summary, p. 6.

## Quotes

> across the OECD, while 40% of firms with 250 or more employees were using AI in 2024 (or in the most recent available year ), only 20.4% of firms of between 50 to 249 employees and only 11.9% of firms with between 10 and 49 employees used AI.

> Among SMEs using generative AI, only 29% report using it in their core activities.

> AI Novices typically rely on embedded tools for peripheral tasks, while AI Optimisers integrate multiple tools across functions. AI Explorers develop bespoke solutions, and AI Champions embed AI across operations and strategy.

> Attitudes towards generative AI did not appear to be a barrier to use with 86% of SMEs reporting either a neutral or favourable attitude within the company .

## Caveats

- **Who paid for it:** written at the request of the 2025 G7 Presidency of Canada, with a voluntary financial contribution from Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (p. 4). It was written to inform a policy blueprint, so it leans towards encouraging adoption.
- **Coverage:** G7 and OECD aggregates; the Netherlands is not reported separately. The generative AI surveys are explicitly non-representative and cover G7 countries only.
- **Reference years vary by country**; definitions differ between national surveys.
