# OECD

The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. In this wiki it is the source of international comparisons of AI adoption by SMEs [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].

- Wrote the 2025 discussion paper on SME AI adoption at the request of the G7 Presidency of Canada, which also made a voluntary financial contribution [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- Runs surveys of SMEs on generative AI in G7 countries; the paper calls two of them non-representative [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
