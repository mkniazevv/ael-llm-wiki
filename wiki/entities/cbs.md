# CBS (Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek)

The Dutch national statistics office. In this wiki it is the source of Dutch figures on business AI use, from its survey table *ICT-gebruik bedrijven* (StatLine) [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].

- Publishes adoption by firm size, purpose, technology and industry, and reasons for not using AI [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Government body with no commercial interest in AI adoption; its releases do not show method, sample or response rate [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Does not report advertising agencies separately; they sit in specialised business services [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
