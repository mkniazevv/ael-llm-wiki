# JPMorganChase Institute

The research institute of the bank JPMorganChase. In this wiki it is the source of US data on which small businesses pay for AI tools, how fast and how much [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].

- Its figures come from payments, so free AI use is not counted [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].
