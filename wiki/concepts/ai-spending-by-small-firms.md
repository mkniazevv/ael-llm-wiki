# AI spending by small firms

How much small firms pay for AI tools. The one source in the wiki shows spending moving towards the cheapest tier as more firms start using AI.

## What the sources say

- In 2025, 63 percent of US small businesses that paid for AI were in the bottom spending tier, 22 percent in the medium tier and 16 percent in the top tier [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].
- Monthly AI spending among small businesses fell after 2022, driven by new users who spend little [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].
