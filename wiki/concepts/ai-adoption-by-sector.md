# AI adoption by sector

How AI use differs between industries. The three sources agree that information and communication and professional or business services are among the leaders, which is where marketing agencies sit.

## What the sources say

- In the Netherlands, 54 percent of businesses in information and communication use one or more AI technologies, up from 28 percent in 2023 [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Specialised business services, which include advertising and market research, use AI relatively often in the Netherlands; no separate figure is given for advertising [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Across G7 countries, ICT and professional services lead adoption [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- Among US small businesses paying for AI, the information sector reached 39.3 percent by the end of 2025 and professional services about 30.3 percent [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].

## Debates

None recorded. The levels differ between sources because they measure different things (any AI use, Netherlands; any AI use, OECD; paid AI tools, US), not because they disagree about the ranking.

## Gap

No source in the wiki gives a figure for marketing or advertising agencies on their own.
