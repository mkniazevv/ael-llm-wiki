# AI adoption barriers

The reasons firms give for not using AI, or the problems they meet when they do. Both sources point to inexperience and legal uncertainty rather than to a negative attitude.

## What the sources say

- Three quarters of Dutch businesses do not use AI and have not considered it [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Of Dutch businesses that considered AI but do not use it, 73 percent name lack of experience as the main reason [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- 49 percent name privacy and 42 percent name legal consequences such as liability; 15 percent say AI is not useful. The base of these figures is unclear in the source [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- SMEs in the G7 that use AI report problems with accuracy, harmful content and legal uncertainty [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- Attitude does not appear to be a barrier: 86 percent of SMEs in an OECD survey reported a neutral or favourable attitude to generative AI [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].

## Debates

None recorded. Both sources name legal uncertainty; neither finds attitude to be the main obstacle.
