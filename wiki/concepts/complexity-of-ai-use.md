# Complexity of AI use

How technically deep and how widespread a firm's AI use is, from a feature built into everyday software to a system built for the firm. The OECD uses it to sort SMEs into four kinds of adopter.

## What the sources say

- The OECD defines complexity of AI use as the technical depth and purpose of the tools, running from AI features embedded in everyday software and off-the-shelf models to tailored systems and frontier AI [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- Its taxonomy has four kinds of adopter: AI Novices use embedded tools for peripheral tasks, AI Optimisers combine several tools across functions, AI Explorers build bespoke solutions, and AI Champions use AI across operations and strategy [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- Because of limited resources, many firms turn to off-the-shelf models rather than building their own [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
