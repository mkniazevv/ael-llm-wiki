# Generative AI use in SMEs

How small firms use AI that writes text or makes images, and how deep that use goes. The sources agree it is the most common kind of AI small firms use or pay for, but mostly outside their core work.

## What the sources say

- In two non-representative OECD surveys of G7 SMEs (2023 and 2024), generative AI was the most used type of AI; the OECD suggests low cost and skill barriers help, at least for basic uses [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- Among SMEs using generative AI, only 29 percent use it in their core activities [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- Generative AI makes up the largest share of paid AI services in every US industry shown, 54 percent in professional services [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].
- The share of US generative AI users who pay every month rose to 30.8 percent in 2025 [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].
- In the Netherlands, 8 percent of businesses use natural language generation and 12 percent use text mining; CBS does not use the term generative AI [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].

## Debates

- **Is natural language generation the same as generative AI?** CBS reports natural language generation at 8 percent [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]]; OECD and JPMorganChase talk about generative AI [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]] [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]]. The CBS category may be narrower (text and speech only) or defined differently. Do not compare the numbers directly.
