# AI for marketing and sales

Firms using AI for their own marketing or sales work. In the Netherlands this is the most common purpose of AI use.

## What the sources say

- Of Dutch businesses that use AI, 35 percent use it for marketing or sales, more than for any other purpose; 32 percent use it for administration or management tasks and 4 percent for logistics [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Almost half of businesses in trade, and about 40 percent in information and communication and in rental and other business services, use AI for marketing or sales [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].

## What this does not show

These are firms doing their own marketing, not agencies doing it for clients [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]]. For an agency it describes its clients more than itself. *(Interpretation by the team, not from a source.)*
