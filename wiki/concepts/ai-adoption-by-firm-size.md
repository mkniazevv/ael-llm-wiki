# AI adoption by firm size

How the share of firms using AI differs between small and large firms. All three sources agree that smaller firms use AI less, but they measure it differently.

## What the sources say

- Across the OECD, 11.9 percent of firms with 10 to 49 employees used AI, against 20.4 percent with 50 to 249 and 40 percent with 250 or more (2024 or the latest year) [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]].
- In the Netherlands, AI use rose fastest for firms with 50 to 250 persons employed, from 20 percent in 2023 to 45 percent in 2025 [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- The CBS table row for 10 to 50 persons employed most likely reads 27 percent in 2025 (provisional), 19 percent in 2024 and 11 percent in 2023; the capture lost the column breaks, so this needs checking [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]].
- Among US small businesses that pay for AI, firms with higher revenue adopt at higher rates, and employer firms adopt more than firms without employees [[undated-jpmc-understanding-the-use-of-ai-among-small-businesses]].

## Debates

- **What share of 10 to 50 person firms use AI?** OECD gives 11.9 percent for 10 to 49 employees [[2025-oecd-ai-adoption-by-small-and-medium-sized-enterprises]]; CBS appears to give 27 percent for 10 to 50 persons employed in the Netherlands in 2025 [[2025-12-12-cbs-bedrijven-gebruiken-ai-vaakst-voor-marketing-of-verkoop]]. The difference turns on country (OECD average against the Netherlands), year (2024 or earlier against provisional 2025), the size definition (employees against persons employed) and an unverified table reading. Do not quote either as the rate for Dutch agencies.
