## Question

Which part of a small marketing agency's work should it test AI on first? This is the business decision behind the team's handbook, asked of the wiki on 11 October 2026.

## Answer

**Text work, with ready-made tools, outside the core client work.**

- Text is where most firms start: the AI technologies Dutch businesses use most are text mining and natural language generation [[generative-ai-use-in-smes]], and marketing or sales is the most common purpose of AI use [[ai-for-marketing-and-sales]].
- Small firms mostly use off-the-shelf tools rather than building their own, and the first stage of adoption is embedded tools for peripheral tasks [[complexity-of-ai-use]].
- Most SMEs that use generative AI keep it out of their core activities (29 percent use it there) [[generative-ai-use-in-smes]], so a first test outside the core work matches what other firms do.
- The main barrier is lack of experience, followed by privacy and legal uncertainty, not attitude [[ai-adoption-barriers]]. A first test should build experience without putting client data at risk.
- It does not need a big budget: most small firms that pay for AI are in the lowest spending tier (US data) [[ai-spending-by-small-firms]].
- Agencies are in the sectors that adopt AI most, so their clients and competitors are likely to be using it already [[ai-adoption-by-sector]].
