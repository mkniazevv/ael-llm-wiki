AI checks:
- **transparency:** concern — Headline figures are given and the underlying table is named (P98), but the captured text contains no method, sample, survey population, response rate or category definitions. Industry groupings are named but not defined by classification code. The StatLine metadata should be consulted.
- **comparability:** concern — '10 tot 50 werkzame personen' (P6) counts persons employed, which may differ from the project's '10 to 50 employees', and it is unclear whether the class includes 50. Advertising is grouped with accountancy, market research and industrial design in specialist business services (P64, P68), so the 31% for that group is not an advertising-agency rate. 'AI technologies' is broader than generative AI. The figures are national, not Gelderland. 2025 is provisional while earlier years may be final. These survey-based rates are not directly comparable with payment-based adoption measures (RUN-003) or unweighted OECD averages (RUN-002).
- **traceability:** concern — Text passages (P1, P19, P20, P42, P64, P96) can be located by paragraph ID. Table rows were flattened during capture, so labels and values run together (for example P4 '17138', probably 17 / 13 / 8; P6 '271911', probably 27 / 19 / 11). This reading matches P1 for the 50–250 class (45% and 20%), but each value needs checking on the live page or in StatLine. Each table appears twice (for example P2–P9 and P10–P17), which is likely a capture artefact.
- **cross_checking:** needed_not_done — Figures used as core evidence should be checked against the StatLine table (P98), and it should be checked whether final 2025 figures have replaced the provisional ones. This is the same page (same title, URL and text) as SRC-004, RUN-001 and RUN-004, and the translation step reported RUN-005 as the same text too. These records are one source, not independent confirmations. EU or OECD figures for the Netherlands may come from the same national survey and would also not be independent.
